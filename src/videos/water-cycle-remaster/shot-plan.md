# Shot plan — `DripWaterCycleRemaster` (camera, planes, light, acting, bubbles per scene; text only, drafted 2026-11-14 from `water-cycle/script.md` + `brief.md` §3/§4 + `docs/ANIMATION-ROADMAP.md` Step 2; showrunner reviews before Build)

Purpose: one row per scene so a builder can stage the picture without re-deciding camera, light or bubble placement; the story, lines, line keys, scene order and the `gaps`/`tailFrames` in `water-cycle/Video.tsx` SCRIPT are frozen — this plan re-pictures the ep-1 beats (SloshScene cards, liftoff + RisingDrops, BigWordBeat/CutFlash, NameArrow, WaterBand, SteamWisps, lever, Kevin, MYTH stamp), it invents no story.
How to read a row: `id` = SCRIPT id; `camera` ties every move to a line key or a word in it; `planes` = brief §3 depth factors (∞ sky, 0.15 far, 0.4 mid, 1.0 near/characters, 1.3 foreground); `light` = brief §4 row + any per-scene exception; `bubbles` = where speech bubbles sit so they never cover a sign, a face or the key light; `V-risk` = the verifier dimension (Step 2 rubric V1–V8) the scene is most likely to fail.
Camera verbs, numbers quoted from Step 2: **HOLD+creep** — "push-in 1.00→1.02 over 6 s, or drift ≤0.3 px/frame; holds ≥70% of episode frames" (a 1.02 creep over 180 f moves the frame edge ≈0.11 px/frame, inside the <0.5 px/frame hold definition).
**PUSH** — "push-in 1.00→1.06 over the reveal on every Big Word"; here 24 f ending on the slam, tilt 0°, no creep while the card is read.
**BURST** — "ease-out burst: peak velocity on frame 1 of the move, decaying to <0.5 px/frame within 8–12 frames (reference 720p px/frame 6, 26, 12, 14, 6, 5, 2, 1 scaled ×1.5 for 1080p)"; "14 px pk" = small, "26 px pk" = big, decay 10–12 f.
**FOLLOW** — "for transitions where an object carries the eye (Drip's liftoff, the slide, the river)"; **DISSOLVE** — "seams between scenes = 6–8 frame dissolve or a continuous move; hard cuts ≤1 per scene; CutFlash stays for Big Words"; **dutch** — "2–4° only in drama beats (slosh, liftoff, check-out slide, the moose)"; low camera (horizon in the top third) when Drip is small among big things.
Readability rules (beat any camera idea): a BURST lands in the 12 f BEFORE a bubble pops or on a VO/sound-word beat; the camera is still while any bubble is read; never tilt during a Big Word card (de-tilt finishes ≥12 f before the slam); bubbles are paper-cream and part of the grade, never white, never over a sign, a face or the key light (ep-1 bugs: s22 bubble covered the CLOUD HOTEL sign; s27 Drip had no waterline in the river — both fixed below).
Light: Sunny IS the key when on screen, the key stays where he last was when he is off; a ground shadow sits under every standing body except s21; "N: VO" = Narrator is voice only (no bubble; if a caption band is used it sits bottom-centre, never over a ground shadow).
