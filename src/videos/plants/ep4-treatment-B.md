# EP 4 — Treatment B: "THE SUMMONS"

Story-writer draft B from `ep4-brief.md`. Binding angle for this draft: the
episode is a **reunion engine** — a hero who cannot take one step must get
the three previous heroes to come to HER, so each act is a recruitment set
piece. The spine is her campaign against the one obstacle: everything she
needs is managed, claimed, or bragged over by Sunny — the only character in
the world who does not impress her. The series-level gag is that everyone
spends the episode trying not to tell Sunny he is essential, until the
mechanism itself forces the admission. This treatment is built to win on
reply-density: characters answering each other on shared ground.

---

## 1. Logline + title candidates

**Logline.** A seed who cannot move — and who is the one character in the
world not impressed by the Sun — summons the show's three heroes, one per
act, to build the biggest thing in the world from her one spot of dirt. The
recipe works: water arrives, air trades, light lands. But every delivery
route runs through the same enormous, delighted supplier, and when the
kitchen finally needs its power switched on, the mechanism makes her say
the one sentence nobody in the episode would say out loud.

**Title candidates** (pattern: hero's name + story noun):

1. **"Pip and the Sunshine Kitchen"** — preferred. Names the mechanism
   (the leaf is a kitchen powered by sunlight), sounds warm, and "kitchen"
   is a noun a six-year-old owns.
2. "Pip and the Tree Made of Air" — the mind-blower as title; spoils the
   Act-3 reveal a little, but it is the Math-bait title.
3. "Pip and the Biggest Thing in the World" — the want as title.
4. "Pip and the Three Deliveries" — the structure as title; flattest of
   the four.

**Hero name.** Keeping the brief's working name **Pip** — one hard
syllable, chantable, seeds a clean roll-call family. Alternatives logged
per brief: *Poppy* (prettier, softer — loses the clipped-boss sound),
*Bean* (funny, but a bean is a food joke we don't need), *Sesame* (cute,
two-syllable, weaker in a roll call), *Dot* (deadpan-appropriate but
collides visually with "a speck" = Drip's old gag territory).

## 2. Hero spec

- **Name:** Pip. A seed, she/her. Dandelion-style: she arrives by air
  (free ep-2 callback) and then never moves again.
- **Want:** to build the biggest thing in the world. Not to see it, not to
  visit it — to BE it, from one spot she will never leave. The want is
  loud, declarative, and fully expressible from a fixed position: she is
  not waiting for anything, she is RUNNING A BUILD.
- **Attitude:** a tiny site manager. She treats the sky as a supply chain
  and the meadow as her lot. She issues summonses, takes deliveries,
  inspects goods, signs for rain. Crucially: she is the one character in
  the series who is not impressed by Sunny — not hostile, never unkind,
  just flatly, magnificently unimpressed. (She never claims he is *wrong*
  — Sunny stays fully right all episode; her whole rebellion is refusing
  to clap.)
- **Comic register (state for the audition):** brisk, bossy, small-but-
  certain — a six-year-old CEO. Two gears: clipped flat deadpan for every
  Sunny beat (slow, unseasoned), and bright command energy for the
  summonses ("command" reads as delight, not temper). New MiniMax voiceId;
  hardest audition lines will be a flat two-word refusal, a shouted
  work-order, and the Act-3 admission (which must be warm, not defeated).
- **Motion law:** **once planted she cannot move — ever — and her motion
  vocabulary is only what a plant honestly does:**
  - **Lean.** Heliotropism is her involuntary tell: whenever she says
    something dismissive about Sunny, her stem drifts toward him and she
    snaps it back. The body betrays the attitude, and it is true botany.
    (Running gag — see §4.)
  - **Grow.** Growth is her only travel; every growth spurt is a
    capability unlock, not a time-lapse. Seed → sprout (she gains height)
    → first leaf (she gains a POINTER — her first "arm", unlocked
    mid-episode; before the leaf she can only lean at things).
  - **Tremble.** Leaf-shiver for reactions.
- **How stuck-in-one-spot is played:** as a choice, loudly. Her cold-open
  reframe — moving is for things too small to stay — sets the register:
  stuckness is never a lack, it is headquarters. Every classic quest beat
  is inverted: she does not go to the water, the water is summoned; she
  does not climb to the light, the light commutes. The comedy shape of the
  whole episode is a hero with total ambition and zero legs, conducting.
  (Arc law honoured: she never counts time, never says "hurry", is never
  bored — waiting comedy stays banked for ep 5.)

## 3. Act-by-act beat sheet

Volcano staging note, binding from S1: the volcano sits on the measured
horizon of the meadow's wide shots from the first frame and stays
continuously visible in every wide thereafter. Nobody looks at it, points
at it, or stings it. Its one stir is in Act 2 (S11).

### COLD OPen

- **S1 — The breath.** Meadow, morning. The kid (silhouette, silent,
  four-episode streak) picks a dandelion clock and BLOWS. Seeds scatter on
  the kid's breath and the wind — Pip's first lines are mid-flight, and
  she is already issuing instructions to the wind ("Left. LEFT."). Ep-2
  callback carried lightly by the Narrator (wind is air in a hurry — one
  clause, not a lesson). NOTE the circle this plants: the episode opens
  with a child giving a plant a breath of air; it closes with a plant
  giving the breath back (S27).
- **S2 — Planted.** The ride ends: she lands in soil, settles in. Tries
  to take a step. Nothing. Tries the other direction. Nothing. Assesses
  her one square inch — and declares the want: she will build the biggest
  thing in the world, *here*. Narrator states the law flatly (plants do
  not move); Pip's reframe lands as the hero thesis — load-bearing line:
  **"Moving is for things that are too small to stay."**
- **S3 — The one non-fan.** Sunny rises: **"GOOD MORNING, EVERYBODY!"** →
  **"I invented mornings!"** (the series-constant greeting+brag pair,
  fired). The whole meadow turns to him — every flower leans. Pip does
  not. Held beat on the one upright stem in a field of leaning ones.
  Sunny, who cannot perceive non-admiration, reads her stillness as awe
  ("Speechless! They often are."). Lean gag planted: her stem drifts
  toward him anyway; she snaps it back. Held beat, unseasoned.

**TITLE CARD.**

### ACT 1 — THE LIGHT CONTRACTOR (recruitment: light / Ray)

- **S4 — The shopping list.** Pip lays out the build: a plant makes
  itself out of three deliveries — LIGHT, WATER, AIR (bubbles/cards).
  Sunny overhears "light" and swells: light is HIS department, always has
  been — **"You're welcome! HA! HA!"** (firing 1). Pip, flat: she will be
  sourcing her light *independently*. Narrator deadpan on the concept of
  independent light. The series gag ignites: the episode's engine is now
  visibly "how long can she avoid the obvious supplier."
- **S5 — The moonlight workaround.** Night. Pip's plan A: work nights,
  hire the Moon, avoid Sunny entirely. A beam arrives in the moonlight —
  **Ray**, entrance staged on his own catch-phrase: **"Look up. That's
  me."** (firing 1). Narrator: **"Different show. Same beam."** (0.92
  flat, fresh blank). Pip hires him on the spot — and Ray, a pedant,
  cannot accept the job without full disclosure: moonlight is not the
  Moon's light; it is Sunny's light, bounced (ep-3 Moon control
  experiment, redeployed as a plot point). **Vindication #1** — Ray's
  first correction of the episode lands in Sunny's favor, which he finds
  structurally upsetting. Pip retains him anyway on a technicality —
  load-bearing punchline: **"You bounced. That makes you freelance."**
  Ray's episode-long tic is born here: **"That is also true."** (see §4).
- **S6 — Blue cameo (+ Violet garnish).** Dawn. The first direct beam
  arrives ricocheting — **Blue**: "First! Sorry, dandelion! I am FIRST!"
  (apologises to the thing he hits, per law). He is wrong twice at once:
  Ray, out-pedanting him, arrived last night via the Moon — and Pip,
  flatter still, delivers the resident's trump, load-bearing: **"I live
  here."** Blue is not corrected further and is not right about anything
  (anti-Sunny seed watered, payoff untouched); he ricochets off. Behind
  the whole beat, **Violet** stands at frame edge, vibrating, noticed by
  no one — zero lines, same body as ep 3.
- **S7 — Light with nowhere to land.** Ray on the job: delivering
  faithfully to Pip's square inch. Nothing happens — she has no leaf yet;
  his light lands and bounces off. Ray files an honest report (light is
  arriving; the customer is not open for business). Discovery that turns
  the act: light is useless until she GROWS the catcher — and growing
  starts with water. New summons target. (Pedagogy honesty: light is not
  consumed by dirt; the green catcher must exist first.)
- **S8 — The puddle that would not walk.** Her water problem, staged: a
  puddle sits in plain sight, one step away, and she cannot take the
  step. She commands it to come; the puddle declines by existing. Ray
  offers help — and being light, cannot carry anything (no arms at rest,
  by design) — but he CAN move water the only way light moves anything:
  UP. He warms the puddle; it shrinks into the sky before her eyes.
  Pip: "You sent my water AWAY." Ray: it will come back down.
  Eventually. Somewhere. Act pivot: she must summon the *rain itself*.
  Sunny, from on high, identifies whose elevator that was — **"You're
  welcome! HA! HA!"** (firing 2). **Vindication #2.**

### ACT 2 — THE WATER DELIVERY (recruitment: water / Drip)

- **S9 — The rain campaign.** How do you make rain come to YOU? Narrator:
  rain comes from clouds, and clouds are full of guests. The Cloud Hotel
  drifts onto the horizon.
- **S10 — Cloudia's checkout (one scene, cameo).** Grand hotel checkout:
  full capacity, guests departing, "darling" on every composed line. The
  delivery joke the brief asked for: Cloudia runs the rain as a parcel
  service — she adores her guests and despises gravity, so checkout is
  her handing each beloved guest to her nemesis, with distaste, invoice
  attached ("Your delivery, darling. Signature required.") Pip, below,
  signs for the weather with a leaf she does not have yet — she signs by
  leaning.
- **S11 — DRIP ARRIVAL (set piece) + the volcano stir.** Drip rides the
  first drop down: **"Hi! It's me! I'm the weather!"** (third identical
  firing — her standing entrance). Narrator: **"Different show. Same
  drop."** (0.92 flat, fresh blank). On-stage reunion with Ray — rain and
  light in one frame; one nod to the rainbow partnership, not a re-fire.
  **THE VOLCANO STIR (wordless, this shot):** the rain wide crosses the
  whole horizon — and where it crosses the volcano's summit, one thin
  curl of steam rises, holds two seconds, and is gone. Rain steams off
  the mountain because the mountain is *warm now*. Nobody looks, nobody
  comments, no sting; the rain just keeps falling. (Escalation rep
  between ep-3's one eye and ep-5's wake: the tell is thermal, honest,
  and entirely deniable.)
- **S12 — The straw.** Drip soaks in and gets pulled UP the root — the
  interior waterslide, upward. Drip, who wants to be taken as big,
  discovers she is now the *inside* of the biggest thing in the world
  ("I'm the plumbing!" register — thrilled, dramatic). On screen: Pip
  GERMINATES. Sprout pops; first growth spurt; first height. Drip claims
  the moment entirely — that was water, water is the whole show.
- **S13 — Credit allocation, reprise.** Ray cannot let a mis-allocation
  stand: he gives Drip exactly her half — the sprout is water's, but the
  lift that put the water in the sky was light's, and the light was...
  Drip and Ray each start the sentence and SWERVE (the not-saying-it gag,
  first ensemble firing: "powered by — the sky. By upstairs. By a large
  warm colleague."). Sunny hears everything: **"You're welcome! HA!
  HA!"** (firing 3). Ray, flat, upset: **"That is also true."**
  (**Vindication #3.**)
- **S14 — ROLL-CALL, firing 1 (greeting).** The rain watered everybody:
  the whole dandelion packet sprouts at once in a row beside her — the
  picture the scene already has. Pip, cheerfully: "Hi Pippa! Hi Pipley!
  Hi other Pip! Hi Pipsqueak! Hi Pip the third!" Narrator, flat middle
  line: "Every seed in the packet was called Pip." Unbothered, unseasoned
  button: Pip, already turning back to the build — "Back to work."
  Nobody replies inside the naming.
- **S15 — Nobody eats dirt + the leaf unlock.** Drip, from inside,
  hazards the audience's wrong model: so... you eat mud? ("I have BEEN
  mud. I was delicious.") Pip, flat: **"Nobody eats dirt."** Soil stays
  an unexplained given — Pip's full account of it: "It's where I keep my
  feet." Then the FIRST LEAF unfurls: gesture unlock (she can point now;
  her first act as a being-with-an-arm is to point at the sky and take
  delivery). Ray inspects the leaf, one pedagogy beat in his ep-3
  mechanics: green is the light-catching stuff — the green you see is
  the bit of his light the leaf bounces back. The kitchen exists.
- **S16 — Act break on an entrance.** Kitchen inventory: water inside ✓,
  light on retainer ✓ — third ingredient: air. Where does one ORDER air?
  The leaf trembles. A voice from nowhere: **"You can't see me. But you
  can FEEL me."** (Puff — exactly once, and it IS his entrance.)

### ACT 3 — THE AIR EATER, AND THE KITCHEN (recruitment: air / Puff → assembly)

- **S17 — The ingredient that RSVPs first.** Puff reveal: air is the one
  ingredient that was already delivered — everywhere, always, before
  being asked. Puff has been at this meadow the whole episode ("I'm
  always here. I'm the guest that lives at the party."). The one summons
  that answers before it is sent — Pip's supply chain finally beats
  Sunny to something, except (Narrator, gently, for later) the wind that
  stirs him is... nobody finishes that sentence either.
- **S18 — The plant EATS air.** First kitchen test on Ray's beam: the
  leaf inhales — and Puff feels a tiny invisible part of himself go IN.
  "The plant is EATING me?!" The trade negotiation (Puff↔Pip
  two-hander): she takes only a tiny invisible part (the fizzy bit —
  carbon dioxide, named lightly or not at all) and gives back a
  different part (the fresh bit — oxygen). Nothing stolen: a trade. Puff
  tests it — breathes at the leaf, gets a breath back — and flips from
  alarm to ecstatic commerce: "I TRADED with a PLANT!"
- **S19 — Mind-blower #1 + rule stamp.** What is she building the trunk
  and branches OUT OF? Not dirt — the soil barely loses any weight. The
  tree's actual body is built from the traded air and the water. Puff,
  awed, in delighted horror→pride: "She's going to be made of ME. I'm
  going to be a TREE." Rule stamp card: **A TREE IS MADE OF AIR.**
  (Math-bait; true and checkable.)
- **S20 — One crumb of sugar.** Full test: water + air + Ray's one beam
  → the kitchen produces its first food. One crumb of sugar. It WORKS —
  and it is tiny. Pip scales it: at one crumb per beam, the biggest
  thing in the world will take approximately forever. Ray, the pedant,
  performs the episode's most upsetting correction ON HIMSELF: one beam
  is not enough; a tree needs the whole sun, all day, every day. Then
  the credit-allocation device fires in its signature form — the
  speaker giving away his own credit — load-bearing, held in silence:
  **"It is mostly him."**
- **S21 — The stall.** Everyone knows what she has to do. Nobody will
  say it. Three swerves in a row, escalating: Drip starts and veers
  ("You could ask — a cloud."), Puff starts and veers ("You could ask —
  the sky? The sky's... roommate?"), Ray gets furthest before physics
  stops him ("You could ask the source of all light in the—" [stops]
  "—no."). Pip's stem leans toward Sunny. This time she does not
  correct it. Held beat.
- **S22 — THE ADMISSION (spine scene — held beats live here).** Pip
  addresses Sunny directly, and it is constructed as a HIRE, not a
  surrender — she keeps her register to the end: no compliments, just
  the true mechanism, stated flat and complete: she cannot build it
  without him; nobody can; every plant, every leaf, every apple that
  ever grew ran on him. Direction note: Sunny — for the only time in
  four episodes — does not interrupt. He lets her finish (his one gift
  back; this is what keeps the scene warm and the admission a gift, not
  a comeuppance). Then the peak, at full unbearable wattage: "I know."
  [beat] **"You're welcome! HA! HA!"** (firing 4), and a short brag aria
  — every forest, every flower, every salad, all of it his kitchen's
  power — with Ray flatly confirming each claim, upset: **"That is also
  true."** (final firing of his chain). Every word of the aria is
  legitimate. Maximum ego, fully earned, zero wrongness.
- **S23 — SUNRISE FLOOD + THE BIG WORD.** Full sun hits the leaf; the
  kitchen ROARS; growth spurt — Pip shoots up past the sprout row,
  conducting. **BigWordBeat: PHOTOSYNTHESIS** — the five-syllable chant
  monster taken one syllable per hero: PHO (Drip) — TO (Puff) — SYN
  (Ray) — THE (Pip) — SIS (Sunny), then all together off the card. (The
  ensemble chant is the reunion engine made audible.)
- **S24 — Sweetness is stored sunshine (mind-blower #3, Sunny's to
  carry).** The sugar payoff: sugar is sunlight, stored — eating an
  apple is eating sunshine. Delivered as Sunny's most legitimate brag
  ever ("Sweetness is me, in a box."). Narrator, echoing her ep-1
  formulation: "Annoyingly... that is completely true."
- **S25 — The button.** Pip, taller now, register intact — one flat line
  that concedes the mechanism without surrendering the attitude
  (shape: she is now his biggest customer, not his fan). Her stem leans
  at him, permanently, and she lets it. Deadpan stillness; unseasoned.

### RECAP + END CARD

- **S26 — Chant.** Three-ingredient chant (WATER! AIR! LIGHT!) with each
  hero re-firing their ingredient in their own register, then the
  PHOTOSYNTHESIS chant reprised off the card.
- **S27 — Mind-blower #2 (the closer, the circle).** The kid silhouette
  returns and takes a breath. Narrator: the oxygen in that breath was
  breathed out by a plant. The cold open closes: the kid's breath
  launched Pip; a plant hands the breath back. Puff carries the
  hand-off ("A plant made that one. Pass it on.").
- **S28 — ROLL-CALL, firing 2 (goodbye) + end card.** Dusk over the
  sprout row. Pip, cheerfully: "Bye Pippa. Bye Pipley. Bye other Pip.
  Bye Pipsqueak. Bye Pip the third." Narrator, flat: "Nobody was going
  anywhere." Unbothered button: "See you tomorrow." — a goodbye between
  permanently planted neighbours: the joke is also the lesson (plants
  stay; they meet the world by making it come to them). End card,
  next-time question: **"What is dirt actually made of?"** (soil bridge
  armed for ep 5; volcano on the horizon, asleep, promising nothing).

## 6. Recap + tease plan

- **Chant:** WATER! AIR! LIGHT! — each hero re-fires their ingredient in
  register (Drip dramatic, Puff commercial, Ray allocative, Sunny
  powered) — then the PHOTOSYNTHESIS syllable chant reprised off the
  card. The recap is a curtain call because the episode is a reunion.
- **Mind-blower slot:** #2 (your next breath) staged on the returning kid
  silhouette — closes the cold-open circle (breath out → breath back).
- **Tease / next-time question:** **"What is dirt actually made of?"** —
  the soil bridge, armed exactly as arc canon wants it: ep 4 used soil as

## 7. Estimated runtime and where the risk is

**Estimate: 14–16 minutes.** 28 scenes against ep-3's ~36-scene / 17:27
benchmark, with a heavier reply-density per scene but shorter diagram
stretches (the mechanism is carried almost entirely by bodies on stage;
the only drawn-geometry candidates are the S4 shopping list and the S20
crumb-math, both of which have two comedians inside them per the
diagram-sag rule).

**Risks, ranked:**

1. **Act 1 carries light that cannot yet be used.** The recruitment is
   real but the ingredient doesn't fire until Act 3 — if the moonlight
   beat (S5) and Blue cameo (S6) don't carry, Act 1 sags. Mitigation is
   already structural (S7 and S8 are two-handers with discoveries, not
   status scenes) and the fallback is cheap: fold S7 into S8.
2. **The admission scene's tone.** S22 must be a gift, not a comeuppance
   (world rule 11) — the hero concedes the mechanism, never her dignity.
   The two protections are built in: Sunny's one non-interruption, and
   the admission being staged as a HIRE in her own register. If it plays
   as defeat in the animatic, the fix is Pip's button (S25), not S22.
3. **Cast cost / reply-density budget.** Six MiniMax voices plus a new
   hero, and the treatment's thesis is replies. If runtime balloons, the
   cuts come from texture beats (Sunny-misreads-awe firings, the S23
   optional "Look up"), never from the engines in §4.
4. **The five-voice Big Word chant** is a production novelty (five clips
   + card sync). If it's fussy, fallback is Narrator-led chant with the
   cast on the reprise only.

## 8. Deviations from the brief, flagged loudly

1. **Puff's entrance skips "Different show. Same ___."** The brief implies
   one firing per returning-character entrance; I fire it twice (Ray
   "Same beam.", Drip "Same drop.") and deliberately NOT for Puff —
   because his entrance is his catch-phrase ("You can't see me. But you
   can FEEL me." — an invisible character cannot be announced by a
   narrator pointing at him, and the rhyme fired three times in one
   episode starts to spend the device). I read the bible's "one per
   entrance" as a rate cap, not a quota; if the showrunner wants the
   third firing, "Different show. Same puff." slots into S17 cleanly.
2. **Cloudia's optional cameo is SPENT** (S10, one scene, the rain
   delivery joke the brief sanctioned). Rationale: in a reunion-engine
   treatment she is a fourth returning face at near-zero cost, and
   delivery is literally her ep-2 verb. No arc, one scene, exits with
   the hotel.
3. **Light is recruited in Act 1 but consumed in Act 3.** The brief's
   cast table maps one ingredient per act; my acts each stage one
   recruitment (light / water / air) but the light *ingredient* pays off
   at the climax, because germination-before-photosynthesis is the
   honest botany AND it puts the Sunny admission at the top of Act 3.
   All three arrivals are still full scenes with entrances and
   reactions, per brief.
4. **Roll-call firing 1 lands in Act 2 (S14), not Act 1.** Precedent
   (eps 2–3) fires the greeting in Act One; here the picture the gag
   needs — a row of identical sprouts — only exists after the rain.
   Flagging the pattern drift explicitly; I think the picture is worth
   it.
5. **New material for an existing character:** Ray's 3-firing `sameAs`
   chain "That is also true." (his vindication tic). It is inside his
   canon register (pedant; corrections as gifts) but it is a new
   construction, so it needs showrunner sign-off before the script round
   treats it as his.
6. **A third mind-blower is placed** (sugar = stored sunlight, S24,
   Sunny's) beyond the required two — it doubles as Sunny's peak brag,
   so it costs no extra runtime; cut it last.
7. **Hero name kept as Pip**; alternatives logged in §1 per brief
   (Poppy / Bean / Sesame / Dot), none preferred.
8. **Sunny's banked wake-claim and "That is not me." are NOT spent** —
   aligned with the showrunner's stated lean, no argument mounted; the
