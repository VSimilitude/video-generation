# EP 4 BRIEF — plants / photosynthesis (slug: `plants`)

Showrunner brief for the treatment round. Two story-writers draft rival
treatments from this same brief; the showrunner synthesizes. Nothing here
overrides `docs/kids/BIBLE.md` or `docs/roles/audience.md` "Series arc canon"

## Premise (fixed)

A seed has to gather the three ingredients of photosynthesis — light, water,
air — and the three ingredients turn out to be the three previous heroes of
the series: Ray (ep 3), Drip (ep 1), Puff (ep 2). The capstone episode of
light + water + air. Sunny's PEAK: plants are his one fully-legitimate claim,
and this is maximum ego before the ep-5 fall (do not fire any wrongness).

## The hero (new character — the only new voice allowed)

- A seed. Working name **Pip** (writer may propose better; log alternatives).
- **Arc law (arc canon, binding): ingredients-quest arc, NOT a waiting arc.**
  Waiting/impatience comedy is reserved for the volcano episode. The seed is
  never bored, never counts time. She is BUSY.
- **Staging law (showrunner, fixed): once planted, the seed cannot move.**
  Plants are stuck — that is honest, and it is the comedy. Every prior hero
  travels (Drip rides the cycle, Puff blows, Ray crosses space in 8 minutes);
  this hero conducts the entire quest from one spot, by getting the world to
  come to her. The cold open may include her one ride (seed dispersal — wind,
  a free ep-2 callback) which ends with her planted.
- Attitude required (retro rule: a hero who only reacts is not a comedian).
  Wants and attitude are the treatments' main degree of freedom — but the
  want must be expressible loudly from a fixed position, and must not be
  "wants things to hurry up" (see arc law). Seed spine suggestion (optional):
  the seed is the one character in the world who is NOT impressed by Sunny —
  and the mechanism forces her to admit, out loud, that she cannot live
  without him. Everyone else spends the episode trying not to say it.
- Voice: MiniMax, new voiceId — auditioned at narration stage by the
  showrunner (≤$1). Write her lines to a definite comic register and STATE
  that register at the top of the cast table so the audition has a target.

## Series obligations (all fixed)

- Shape: cold open → title → three acts → recap (chant, mind-blower, tease) →
  next-time question on the end card.
- **One fresh roll-call variant, fired twice** (greeting + goodbye). Obvious
  candidate: the identical seeds she was scattered with / the row of sprouts.
  Shape is fixed: cheerful naming → one flat Narrator line → unbothered
  unseasoned button. Nobody replies inside the naming.
- **The volcano: one small stir.** Escalation ladder rep between ep-3's
  one-eye and ep-5's wake. Volcano rule binds: scenery, never named in a
  line, measured horizon, continuously visible once established, nobody
  looks at it, points at it, or stings it musically. Propose a stir staging
  (wordless is acceptable and may be strongest). Sunny's banked wake-claim
  ("OH! That one is me as well!") and "That is not me." are AVAILABLE but the
  showrunner leans SAVE for ep 5 — if your treatment spends either here, argue
  for it explicitly.
- **Soil is an unexplained given.** The seed lives in it, drinks through it,
  is never told what it is. (Ep 5 pays off its origin.) The end-card tease MAY
  be "what is dirt actually made of?" — or the volcano stir — propose one.
- Big Word: **PHOTOSYNTHESIS** (one BigWordBeat; it is a five-syllable chant
  monster — that is the fun). At most one more vocab card, and only if earned;
  a *rule* stamp (ep-2 precedent "WARM AIR RISES") is separate and available,
  e.g. "PLANTS MAKE THEIR OWN FOOD" / "A TREE IS MADE OF AIR".
- Mind-blower channel (Math, 8, checks facts): strongest candidates —
  (1) a tree is built mostly out of AIR and water, not soil (the soil barely
  loses weight); (2) the oxygen in your next breath was breathed out by a
  plant; (3) sugar is stored sunlight — eating an apple is eating sunshine.
  Pick and place at least two; they must be true and checkable.

## Pedagogy (physics-honesty checklist — a wrong model is a bug)

- Plants MAKE their own food (sugar) from water + carbon dioxide (a tiny
  invisible part of air), powered by light. They do not eat dirt.
- Oxygen is the leftover, breathed out — and it is OUR every breath.
- Green = the light-catching stuff in the leaf (chlorophyll may be named or
  not; do not card it unless earned).
- Nothing is stolen or destroyed: the air gives a part and gets a part back;
  water comes up the straw; light is used, not taken from Ray. Characters
  carry the mechanism — the gag IS the lesson (Cloudia-stress standard).

## Comedy standard (measured, binding)

- Kid-graded laugh every ≤25–30s; MAX gap ≤50s, counted on SPOKEN lines
  against the eventual cut. Any stretch carried by drawn geometry gets a
  character beat inside it.
- **Count replies, not lines.** Ep-3's lesson: an ensemble whose members
  never answer each other is a diagram with faces. Build recurring
  two-hander engines out of colliding wants (Seed↔Sunny is the obvious
  central engine; find at least two more). Set pieces belong to characters,
  not the narrator; every arrival/exit/incident gets an on-stage reaction.
- Repetition/deadpan gags get room: slower per-line speed, punchlines held
  in silence, buttons unseasoned. Deadpan is stillness — but do not starve
  the episode with wall-to-wall holds; held beats only where they are spine.
- Plan `sameAs` chains at draft time with the EARLIEST firing as source.
- TTS rules: no all-caps emphasis inside MiniMax sentences (caps live in
  bubbles); kokoro-only letter-stretches; pause markers only for scripted
  intra-line timing; split clips for held beats between sentences.
