## Title + hero (ruled)

- **Title: "Pip and the Sunshine Kitchen".** (Logged alternatives: A's
  "World's Biggest Kitchen", B's "Biggest Thing in the World".)
- **Hero: Pip**, dandelion seed, she/her. **Want (merged):** to build the
  biggest thing in the world, from one square inch, by running it as a
  kitchen/site she commands. **Attitude:** tiny site manager; job-title
  runner (A) — she addresses everyone by role, never name ("the delivery
  service", "one water", "the air order"). The one character in the world
  not impressed by Sunny; never claims he is wrong (he never is).
- **Comic register for casting:** clipped, brisk, small-but-certain,
  DRY not loud, warm underneath — "a six-year-old CEO". Hardest audition
  lines: a shouted work-order at the sky, the flat two-word stamp
  "It will do.", the warm-not-defeated admission beat.
- **Motion vocabulary (B, verbatim):** lean (heliotropism), grow
  (capability unlocks: sprout = height, first leaf = her first pointer),
  tremble. Never moves, no camera cheats. Hero thesis line kept:
  **"Moving is for things that are too small to stay."**
