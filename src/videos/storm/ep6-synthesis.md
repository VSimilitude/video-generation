# EP 6 SYNTHESIS — binding rulings (showrunner, 2026-10-26)

Inputs: `ep6-treatment-A.md` ("the hotel is the episode") and
`ep6-treatment-B.md` ("the count is the episode"). Rule of record for the
script: THIS FILE, then the brief, then the bible. Where a treatment detail
isn't ruled on here, the script writer follows the treatment the spine came
from (**B for structure, A for the hotel texture**) and flags any invention
in the deviations ledger.

The two drafts converged on the hero (Boom, he/him, the thunder), his
chain word ("Present!"), Flash as the silent she/her constant, the hail
roll call (Hail, Hailey, other Hail, Hail the third, …Stony), Cloudia

rulings below are where they split.

## 1. The spine: B's structure, A's hotel

Drip rides up and freezes, the hail roll call (greeting), STATIC, the

seconds." / "Keep counting.") — act break → **Act 2 — THE COUNT COMES
DOWN**: gust front + Puff's catch-phrase, six, Blue's payoff at three, the
lesson said once + LIGHT IS FAST stamp, two, one, **zero = THE BREAK**,

THREE COUNTS stamp, nine = the rumble, goodbye roll call on the lawn, **ten

## 2. The break — B's shape, ruled

## 5. The count — rules carried to the script

Kokoro, **ten recordings "One." … "Ten."**, one number per clip, each
written as its own line so the count can shrink and grow; aliases state
intent. **30-frame grid: each number clip + its gap = 30f** — a production
gate, not a taste call (the ruler is only honest if the metronome is).
Counts in order: cold open 9 (SFX rumble, no "Present!") → act-1 cut
"…Seven. Eight. Nine." → act 2: 6, 3 (Blue), 2, 1, 0 (break) → act 3: 3, 6
(turn), 9 (rumble), 10-and-nothing (**120f**, "That is all the fingers." /
"More than fifteen kilometres. That is forty-five counts." / "You would
need four and a half more kids."). **A ten-count flash is never silent**
(3.3 km is audible) — the no-boom flash is the >15 km one and the Narrator
says so (B's guard). The kid's fingers are the only prop; during every
count Boom is VISIBLE rolling across the bay (B — a chase, never a wait).
If the cold-open nine proves long at manifest time, the fallback is six
(B's own fallback) — the cut then joins at "Four. Five. Six."
