# EP 5 TREATMENT A — "The Volcano Wakes" (working title: "Ember and the Slowest Thing Alive")

Stance: **the two-hander is the episode.** Ember versus Rock — the fastest
voice the series has ever cast against the slowest voice it owns — is the
spine, the mechanism delivery system, and the laugh engine. The volcano's
wake and Sunny's ceremony are enormous, and they are both in service of one
small hot drop learning what big actually costs. Geological time is the
antagonist; the middle of the episode is the world's slowest traffic jam,
narrated with love.

Logline: a brand-new drop of lava is born in the world's basement wanting
everything immediately, gets thrown out the front door of a mountain, stalls
next to the most patient character in the series, and discovers — over one
crust, one beach, one island, and one seed — that slow is what big feels
like from the inside.

---

## 1. Why the two-hander carries it (the argument)

1. **The interval is the instrument.** Rock is `Deep_Voice_Man` at 0.85 —
   the slowest read in the cast. Ember is cast at the top of the speed
   range (proposal below: 1.1, the suite's new fastest, dethroning Blue's
   1.05). Every exchange between them is funny before it has content,
   the way Blue↔Red was in ep 3 — except this pair shares every scene of
   two acts instead of colliding in a race. We have never had a sustained
   engine built on maximum register interval. This episode is the one
   place in the series where that pair is also the PEDAGOGY: Ember is
   literally Rock's childhood and Rock is literally Ember's future, so
   every joke between them is the rock cycle saying itself out loud.
2. **Waiting comedy needs a wall.** Ep-3's lesson (STYLE: deadpan needs
   liveliness to push against) cuts both ways: mania needs stillness to
   push against. Rock is the best wall the series owns, and he has been
   sitting in the bible since ep 2 waiting for this job.
3. **The mechanism act is the funniest act by construction, not by
   punch-up.** Lava→rock→crumble→soil is staged entirely as things
   happening TO Ember, graded by Rock, at a tempo Ember finds morally
   offensive. The facts are the jokes: cooling is the gag, the rename is
   the gag, the traffic jam is the gag, ash skipping the queue is the gag.
4. **The volcano and Sunny stay events, not engines.** Scarcity is the
   volcano's character (5 lines, all landed slow); the ceremony is a
   four-episode payoff that needs a quiet stage and an armed audience.
   Both get their maximum value when the two-hander has been carrying the
   cadence and can hand them a silent room.

## 2. The hero

- **Name: Ember** (keeping the working name). Two syllables, warm,
  sayable by a six-year-old, and not pre-spoiled the way "Roxy" would be
  (her third name has to land as a punchline — see the rename runner).
  Pedant note: an ember is strictly burning wood; it is her NAME, not a
  claim, and no line asserts she is one. Alternatives considered and
  dropped: "Splat" (undignified for a hero Claire commissioned), "Zing"
  (no warmth).
- **Gender: girl.** Three reasons. (a) This is Claire's commissioned
  episode and the series' commissioned-hero precedent (Drip, ep 1) is a
  girl at full velocity. (b) Register: the fast-bright male end of the
  MiniMax catalog is effectively spent (`Decent_Boy` is Blue AND Indigo);
  the bright female shelf has more separation room against the four
  collision voices named in the brief. (c) The comedy interval: the
  smallest, brightest, quickest voice in the series against
  `Deep_Voice_Man` at 0.85 is the widest two-hander interval we can buy.
- **Casting spec for the overnight audition (PROVISIONAL, Mike's ear):**
  new MiniMax voice, register target: small, bright, quick, GLEEFUL —
  indignation always played inside delight, never whiny. Speed 1.1
  proposal (new series fastest; the speed is the want, not the physics —
  the irony that the fastest-talking character in the series is made of
  the slowest stuff on the planet IS the arc, in the casting). Cast
  emotion: `happy` throughout, seasoning limited to two `surprised`
  firings (the rename; the turn), stage directions cited at script stage.
  Audition line suggestion: "Why is everyone stopped? Is it broken? Who
  do I talk to?" — it needs speed, opinion, and zero whine.
- **Want:** everything, immediately. Out of the mountain, down the slope,
  to the sea, to be big — today, before lunch.
- **Attitude (hero = comedian):** impatience with OPINIONS. Everything
  slow is "broken", "stuck", or "doing it wrong". She files verdicts, she
  does not ask permission. Her grade for the world is the chain word:
  "Done!" — premature, delighted, and wrong four times.
- **The turn (not saccharine, per brief):** she never learns that
  patience is a virtue. She learns that fast things are small and slow
  things are big, and that rock time is the price tag on being enormous —
  so she re-brands geological time as her own ambition and starts
  speed-running a ten-thousand-year project. Her final state is exactly
  as impatient as her first; the impatience is just aimed at a mountain
  now. Landing line: "Then I have already started."
- **Speed detail:** her final two scenes drop to 1.0 (not slower — this
  is settling, not sedation). The arc is audible.

## 3. The fresh repetition engine (and why it is not the Ray gag re-skinned)

**The double chain: "Done!" / "…Started."**

- Ember: **"Done!"** — 4 firings, ONE recording (`sameAs`, source =
  firing 1, Act 2). A verdict, not a question: she declares things
  finished. Fired at escalating objects: her own crust → the first black
  beach → the growing headland → the surfaced island.
- Rock: **"…Started."** — 4 firings, ONE recording (`sameAs`, source =
  firing 1). Identical every time; the leading ellipsis is his fixed
  24-frame approach gap (see below).
- The escalation is SEMANTIC, not temporal: the silences never grow (that
  was Ray's construction — 5 identical questions, escalating gaps 45→165f,
  spent). Here the gaps are CONSTANT (they are Rock's identity, not the
  gag's mechanism) and what escalates is (a) the size of the thing Ember
  declares done and (b) the Narrator's numbers in between (one day → one
  year → ten thousand years). Ray's gag was one voice asking the world;
  this is two voices grading it — a verdict engine, not a query engine,
  and both halves are byte-identical, which the series has never done.
- **The break (Act 3, scene 23, the episode's biggest constructed laugh):**
  the exchange REVERSES. Rock, for the first time, asks: "…Done?" (new
  recording — the question mark is the break). Ember answers with Rock's
  own word and Rock's own gap: "…Started." (her voice, new recording, at
  1.0). The hero's arc lands as a role reversal inside the gag, in
  silence, with zero moral stated.
- **Rock's approach gap: 24 frames, fixed, every line** — the new series
  record (Red holds 16f). Identity, planned at script stage, never
  escalated.
- Ember's opinion vocabulary ("broken", "stuck", "doing it wrong") stays
  free-range — varied lines, no chain, so the chain stays clean.

## 4. Scene-by-scene (laugh engine named per stretch)

### COLD OPEN

**CO-1 — The first smoke.** Beach, early light, the kid in silhouette
(fifth canonical episode — streak kept). The classic wide the series has
guarded for three episodes. A long low rumble. One fat grey curl of smoke
from the summit — and the kid TURNS HIS HEAD. First on-screen look at the
volcano in series history; the licensed exception begins.
ENGINE: dead-still under-reaction + three episodes of horizon discipline
paying off in one head-turn.

**CO-2 — The wake-claim.** Sunny, instantly, at full confidence, no doubt
anywhere in it (banked direction travels with the line): "OH! That one is
me as well! HA! HA!" Hold. The Narrator — who has answered every claim he
has ever made — says only: "Hm." (0.9). And moves on. 30f.
ENGINE: the withheld verdict. She ALWAYS rules; the pattern break is the
fuse audibly hissing, and a six-year-old hears it.

**CO-3 — The debt.** Narrator: "Last time, somebody asked what dirt is
actually made of. Somebody else said: send it here, I will grade it. That
order is still open. The answer starts a long way down." Camera dives at
the mountain — through the ground — black. TITLE.
ENGINE: the live order from ep 4 named on screen; promise accepted in the
first minute (cashed in Act 3, argued in the deviations ledger).

### ACT 1 — THE BASEMENT

**S1 — The dive (drawn geometry WITH a character beat inside it).**
Cross-section: down through dirt, rock, warmer, warmer — glow. Big Word
card: **MAGMA**. Narrator: "Deep down, the world is so hot that rock is
melted. The world keeps its own heat. It is left over from the day the
world was made. It has nothing to do with the sun. Remember I said that."
The diagram is never empty: a queue of glowing silent drops (Blobby-tier
bodies) waits in it, perfectly content, some holding tiny queue numbers.
ENGINE: the Narrator deputizing the audience ("Remember I said that." —
kids armed for the ceremony, eleven minutes early, the ep-4 riddle trick)
+ the sight gag of a waiting room at the center of the earth.

**S2 — Ember is born.** One drip melts off the chamber ceiling, falls,
lands in the glow — pop: a face, already talking, maximum velocity. First
lines inside ten seconds of existing: "Hello! I am new! Where is the
door? Why is everyone stopped? Is it broken? Who do I talk to?"
ENGINE: newborn at 1.1 versus a room that has not moved in ten thousand
years. Speed-interval comedy with zero replies — the queue's total
stillness IS the wall.

**S3 — Working the queue.** Ember interviews the queue (nobody answers,
nobody minds), diagnoses the establishment: "This place is broken. All of
it. I have been here my whole life and nothing has happened." Narrator:
"Her whole life was, at this point, one minute long." She demands the
manager.
ENGINE: escalating impatience against stillness; the Narrator fact-checking
her lifespan deadpan.

**S4 — THE VOLCANO SPEAKS (series event).** Cut OUTSIDE to the classic
horizon wide — held, silent, 45f. Then, for the first time in five
episodes, the scenery talks. `Elegant_Man`, pitch −6, slow:
V1: "…Good morning, little one. I heard you. I am opening the doors as
fast as I can. Do make yourself comfortable."
45f. Cut back inside: Ember, unimpressed: "Finally. The manager."
ENGINE: the three-season payoff (the mountain that finally roars speaks
like a butler) undercut by the one character in the world too new to know
it is a miracle. The staging does the awe so the hero doesn't have to.

**S5 — The push.** Mechanism, simple and honest: "The inside of the world
pushes. It has been pushing on those doors for ten thousand years. Today
is opening day." Ember, to the walls: "Shortly! You said shortly!"
V2: "Do pardon the ash."
SMASH CUT. Big Word card: **ERUPTION**.
ENGINE: geological courtesy as the fuse on chaos — the politest sentence
in the series used as a starter pistol.

### ACT 2 — THE LONGEST TRAFFIC JAM

**S6 — Opening day.** Night eruption, staged as fireworks and birth —
glorious, zero peril, nothing harmed (tone rule). Ember launched, the
happiest anyone has ever been: "Finally! Everything! All at once!" On the
lower slope, lit orange: ROCK. Narrator: "Different show. Same rock."
(0.92, firing 1 of 2). Rock watches the fountain like a man at his old
school: "Ohh yeah. That is the stuff." (cache migration from ep 2,
firing 1 of 2).
ENGINE: Rock's nostalgia deadpan under the biggest spectacle the series
has drawn — the stillest character reviewing the loudest event.

**S7 — The rename.** Mid-air, mid-joy, the card slams: **LAVA**.
Narrator: "Inside the mountain, melted rock is called magma. The moment it
steps outside — new name. Lava. Same stuff. New name." Ember, indignant at
terminal velocity: "I did not change! Everything else changed!"
ENGINE: bureaucracy gag at maximum speed — the paperwork objection is the
pedagogy (same stuff, new name) said as a complaint.

**S8 — Traffic.** Ember's lava river pours down the slope… and slows. And
thickens. And STALLS — right next to Rock. Ember: "Why are we stopped? Is
it roadworks? It is roadworks, isn't it." Narrator: "It was not roadworks.
It was Tuesday. Lava is slow traffic."
ENGINE: commuter comedy at geological speed — the takeaway ("rock is slow
traffic") introduced as a traffic report.

**S9 — The slowest thing alive.** Introductions. Rock's credential,
24f gap, held after: "…I was lava once." Ember, scanning him top to
bottom: "You? You are the slowest thing I have ever met." Rock:
"…Thank you." (sincere — he means it; slow is what mountains are).
ENGINE: the mentor reveal as a two-beat deadpan; the compliment
misdelivered and gratefully received.

**S10 — The crust ("Done!" 1).** Ember's edges go dark — she is growing a
skin. Panic → verdict: "Done!" (SOURCE recording). Rock: "…Started."
(SOURCE recording). Mechanism ridden by the pair: cooling is hardening,
hardening is rock, rock is new ground. Ember refuses the promotion: "I
decline. I have places to be." Rock, two sentences, 24f between: "…You are
not going somewhere." / "You are becoming somewhere."
ENGINE: chain debut + the episode's thesis delivered as the driest
possible correction. Punchline held 30f.

**S11 — Dawn.** Sunny up, five-for-five on the series constant:
"GOOD MORNING, EVERYBODY!" / "I invented mornings!" He warms the slope,
entirely legitimately. Narrator: "He warms the outside of everything.
Remember that as well." (Second deputizing plant — the audience now holds
both halves of the verdict.)
ENGINE: the Sunny constant + the audience conspiracy; also the quiet
comedy of Sunny beaming at an erupting volcano and suspecting nothing.

**S12 — Where the lava meets the sea ("Done!" 2).** The front of the flow
reaches the water: hiss, steam, and BLACK NEW GROUND. Narrator: "That
beach is one day old. It is the newest ground in the world." Ember stands
on land younger than she is: "Done!" (alias). Rock: "…Started." (alias).
Ember tries to help it along — blows on the lava to cool it faster.
Narrator: "That helped. Nobody can measure how much. But it helped."
ENGINE: chain firing 2 + honest-futility gag (blowing genuinely does
cool it, immeasurably) — the impatient hero's effort graded with perfect
scientific fairness.

**S13 — Eruption by eruption ("Done!" 3).** Time passes in NAMED STEPS,
never a time-lapse (Pip growth-state law applied to geography): jump cuts
between states — new black beach / bigger headland / steaming mound
offshore — each stamped by the Narrator with a number ("one year later" /

you to count."). At a later eruption, Rock, identical take across the
span: "Ohh yeah. That is the stuff." (migrated alias, firing 2 of 2 —
in ep 2 the joke was that nothing happened between firings; here
EVERYTHING happened between them and his reaction is byte-identical: the
constancy is the character). Ember at the mound: "Done!" (alias). Rock:
"…Started." (alias). Two-hander bus-stop business keeps cadence between
beats — the name exchange: Rock: "…You will change names one more time."
/ Ember: "To what?" / 24f / Rock: "…Rock." / Ember: "That is your name."
/ 24f / Rock: "…It is a very popular name." (deliberate ep-2 roll-call
echo, new voice, flagged in the ledger).
ENGINE: identical-take-across-time + escalating numbers + the namespace
gag (all rock is named Rock — the whole geology is a raindrop queue).

### ACT 3 — THE NEW ONE

**S14 — The island ("Done!" 4).** Wide: the mound has surfaced. An

V3: "…Ah. There it is. I do like this part."
30f. ENGINE: the chain's last firing + the true builder's modest pride —
staged one beat before the false claim, so the trap is set in silence.

24f. Narrator, flat, floor speed: "No. He really didn't."
45f — nothing moves.

V4: "…That one was me. I do apologise for the confusion."

Narrator: "A long time ago, I made a promise. One day, Sunny will be

ENGINE: the four-episode payoff. The laughs are the silences, the
volcano's apology, and the recovery — his ego rebuilding in real time,

## 5. Ceremony placement: Act 3, at the island — the argument (from the stance)

The brief leaves placement open: at the eruption vs Act 3. Treatment A
puts the claim-and-verdict at the ISLAND (S15), not the eruption, for
four reasons:

1. **You cannot hold 36 frames of real silence inside an eruption.** The
   long fuse is the series' longest-armed payoff and its stated staging
   requirement is real room. The eruption is the loudest, busiest frame
   we have ever drawn; the surfaced island in still daylight is the
   quietest stage the episode owns. The ceremony gets the silent room;
   the eruption gets to be an eruption.
2. **The verdict must be checkable by the audience before it lands.**
   The physics IS the verdict (world rule 1), and the audience learns
   the inside-heat fact in Act 1 (S1, "Remember I said that.") and
   Sunny's legitimate half in Act 2 (S11, "Remember that as well.").
   By S15 a six-year-old holds both halves and can beat the Narrator to
   "No. He really didn't." — the best version of this beat is the one
   Claire says half a second before the show does. At the eruption,
   the claim would arrive before the audience is armed.
3. **The eruption belongs to the hero.** From this treatment's stance the
   eruption is Ember's birth and Rock's homecoming; parking the series'
   biggest Sunny beat on top of it makes the episode's spine share its
   own climax. Sequenced this way, each of the three giants gets a solo:
   the volcano owns Act 1's turn (first line) and the eruption, the
   two-hander owns the middle, Sunny owns S15.
4. **"I did that." wants a finished-looking object.** Claiming an
   in-progress eruption is claiming a process; claiming a brand-new
   island in the morning sun is claiming a THING — cleaner for a
   six-year-old, and it lets Rock's final "…Started." (S14) sit directly
   under Sunny's claim as dramatic irony: the audience was just told the
   island isn't even done, and here is Sunny declaring it done AND his.
   The wake-claim (cold open, per the decision log's lean) stays where
   the reflex is funniest and leaves the whole episode under an
   unanswered claim — the Narrator's "Hm." keeps the fuse audible for
   fifteen minutes.

Staging invariants honored: verdict pair verbatim; 36f fuse gap; Sunny's
stunned beat is the series' first and nothing enters it; nobody unkind;
recovery AS SUNNY with a true half-claim; his ego survives, only his
infallibility dies.
