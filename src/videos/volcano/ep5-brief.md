## 0. The commission

Claire (6) commissioned "the volcano/lava episode" after ep 1. This is
her episode the way ep 3 was the ensemble experiment: the comedy channel
(deadpan repetition, roll calls) leads. Math (8) gets true/checkable
mind-blowers. Runtime 10–30 min — hit the beats, never target a duration.

## 1. The single takeaway

**The ground is not finished.** Rock is not a forever-thing — it is slow
traffic. The inside of the world becomes new outside (lava → rock), the
outside crumbles (weathering/erosion → sand → soil), and the world is
still being built today, just very, very slowly. The volcano is not a
disaster; it is the world's construction site.

Pedagogy beats (all rideable by characters, world rule 2):
- Deep underground it is so hot rock is melted — MAGMA. (Why: the world
  keeps its own heat inside, left over from when it was made. NOT the
  Sun's — this fact IS the Sunny ceremony, see §4.)
- When it comes out, it gets a new name — LAVA. (Big Word card fodder;
  the same-stuff-new-name gag writes itself.)
- Lava cools and hardens into new rock = brand-new land. Islands are
  built this way, layer by layer, eruption by eruption.
- Mountains crumble: rain, wind, and time break rock into smaller rock,
  then gravel, then sand, then — mixed with old dead leaves — SOIL.
  This pays off ep 4's unexplained given. Ep-4's complete on-screen
  account of dirt was Pip's: "Nobody eats dirt." and **"It's where I
  keep my feet."** — and the ep-4 end card ARMED this episode verbatim:
  card **"WHAT IS DIRT ACTUALLY MADE OF?"**, Narrator "Next time. What
  is dirt actually made of?", Pip "Send it here. I will grade it."
  (episode's last line). **The cold open or the recap must cash that
  promise** — "send it here, I will grade it" is a live order from the
  previous episode; the strongest available button is fresh new-island
  soil actually getting graded (Pip cameo, "It will do." re-fired once
  via cache migration — ep-5 budget, chain source `co_17_pip`).
  Treatments decide where and how; deviating from cashing it at all
  needs an argument.
- The loop closes: new island → first soil → first seed. Rock cycle as
  the series' quiet thesis: everything travels, even the ground.

OUT OF SCOPE (Mike's ruling, final): outgassing / volcanoes making air.
Also keep out: plate tectonics as a named mechanism, sedimentary/
metamorphic rock classes, casualties/destruction framing (tone rule —
the eruption is construction, never catastrophe; nothing on screen is
harmed by it).

Mind-blower candidates for the recap (true + checkable, Math-graded;
synthesis picks 1–2):
- Your fingernails grow faster than mountains rise (Everest ~4–5 mm/yr).
- The next Hawaiian island is already growing underwater RIGHT NOW
  (Kamaʻehuakanaloa) — it just needs a few more ten-thousands of years.
- The longest mountain range on Earth is at the bottom of the ocean.
- Every grain of beach sand used to be part of a mountain.

## 2. The hero — NEW character, impatience arc (arc canon, binding)

The volcano-ep hero must NOT reuse ep-3's wrong-about-self arc; the
designated engine is **impatience vs the slow rock cycle** — waiting
comedy was reserved for this episode across two campaigns. Design:

- **Working name "Ember"** — a brand-new drop of lava (pending treatment
  counter-proposals, same convention as "Pip": one-syllable-ish, sayable
  by a six-year-old; propose alternatives if you have better).
- Born in Act 1 (or the cold open) inside the volcano; the youngest,
  fastest, most NOW character the series has had. Wants: everything,
  immediately. First entrance at maximum velocity; the episode slowly
  teaches the one thing money can't buy in this world: rock time.
- The engine two-hander: **Ember ↔ Rock** (ep-2 Rock, `Deep_Voice_Man`
  0.85, the slowest voice in the series). Rock is the mentor and the
  punchline: he was lava once — Ember is literally Rock's childhood, and
  Rock is Ember's future. The fastest voice in the cast against the
  slowest is the episode's Blue↔Red.
- Hero attitude (heroes are comedians, not reactors): impatience with
  OPINIONS — everything slow is "broken", "stuck", "doing it wrong".
  The arc is not "learns patience is a virtue" (saccharine); it is
  discovering that slow is what BIG feels like from inside — the hero
  wanted to be big (fast things are small things), and rock time is the
  price of being a mountain. Wrongness is never a comeuppance.
- Waiting-comedy construction: needs a NEW shape — Ray's "Are we there
  yet?" (5 firings + escalating silences) is SPENT, per ledger. Build a
  fresh repetition engine (e.g. "Now?" answered by Rock's geological
  "...No." — sameAs chain, escalating gaps — or better). It must be its
  own construction, not the ep-3 gag re-skinned.
- Casting: new MiniMax voice, auditioned by measurement overnight,
  PROVISIONAL, logged for Mike's ear. Fast/bright register that can
  sit at speed ≥1.0 without colliding with Blue's `Decent_Boy`, Drip's
  `Lively_Girl`, Puff's `Exuberant_Girl`, or Pip's `Inspirational_girl`.
  Gender: writer's choice, justify in treatment.

## 3. The volcano — first line ever (series event)

The bible's silent constant finally wakes. Voice RESERVED by Mike:
`Elegant_Man`, pitch −6. Rules:

- He has been on the horizon of every coastal shot since ep 2, never
  named, never looked at. When he wakes, the series pays that off — the
  scenery becomes a character. Treat the first line as the biggest
  single beat in the episode after the Sunny ceremony.
- Character: **elegant**. Enormous, courteous, unhurried, faintly
  apologetic about the noise; geological good manners ("Do pardon the
  ash."). He is not scary and not grumpy — the joke is that the
  mountain that finally roars turns out to speak like a butler.
- SCARCITY IS THE CHARACTER: single-digit line count for the whole
  episode. He was silent for three episodes; his speech stays rare and
  every line lands slow. No treatment gives him banter.
- He never gets a body walk — he is the mountain. Face/gesture staging
  stays within "the mountain moved slightly and that is enormous".
- The sleep-gag ladder ended in ep 4 (final rep fired: the wordless
  Scene-13 steam curl as rain crossed the summit — nobody saw it, nobody
  knows). Ep-4's exact end state, which ep 5 picks up from: "The volcano
  holds the horizon, asleep, promising nothing." No character in-world
  knows anything. Ep 5 does NOT add another sleep beat — the next
  volcano beat is waking.

## 4. Sunny — the wrongness ceremony (three episodes of setup, handle
with white gloves)

Banked, canon, and MUST fire in ep 5:
- Cold-open-adjacent: Sunny's reflexive wake-claim "OH! That one is me
  as well! HA! HA!" (banked since ep 2, CONFIRMED still banked through
  ep 4 — saved on purpose twice; ep-4 decision log leans ep-5 cold open;
  direction note travels with it: full confidence, no doubt anywhere in
  it).
- The ceremony: **"I did that." / "No. He really didn't."** — Sunny's
  first true wrongness, reserved since ep 2.
- The long fuse (ep-2 `a2_45`/`a2_45b`): "One day Sunny will be wrong
  about something." — 36f silence — "It is not today." The narrator gets
  to close it: the shape of "It is today." fired at the ceremony is the
  series' longest-armed payoff. Treatments should stage this with real
  room (comedy-pacing rule: the punchline is held in silence).
- WHY he is wrong, honestly: the volcano's heat is the Earth's OWN
  (left over from when the world was made) — the one heat on the planet
  that is genuinely not Sunny's. The physics IS the verdict (world rule
  1). The "That is not me." inversion is also banked and available.
- Tone guardrails, hard: wrongness is never a comeuppance; nobody is
  unkind; Sunny does not crumble. He takes the verdict with a stunned
  beat (the series' first) and then recovers AS SUNNY — finds the
  half-claim he can legitimately keep (he does warm the OUTSIDE of the
  world; the inside heat is not his) or pivots grandly. His ego survives
  the episode; only his infallibility dies. Placement of the ceremony
  (at the eruption vs act 3) is a treatment decision — argue it.
- Sunny stays kokoro `am_puck`: no emotion field, no pauses, stops
  carried by words and speed.

## 6. Series machinery this episode must serve

- **Roll call** (one fresh variant, fired twice — greeting + goodbye,
  fixed 3-part shape). Candidates, treatments pick/beat them:
  (a) the island chain — hero greets a queue of near-identical islands
  the volcano built, oldest first ("Hi, Isla! Hi, Islet! ...") — the
  roll call IS the lesson (this volcano has done this before, many
  times); (b) identical lava drops in the crater ("other Ember",
  "Ember the third"); (c) identical rocks on the slope. Prefer the one
  whose picture the scene already has and whose joke is the lesson.
- **Catch-phrase ceilings** (bible table, hard): "You're welcome! HA!
  HA!" per-episode ceiling 8 (ep 4 used 5 — placement escalates, the
  read never does); greeting+brag pair is a series constant, now
  four-for-four — fire it; "That one is me as well!" next firing
  BELONGS to the volcano thread (this is it).
- **sameAs discipline**: every repetition gag one recording, earliest
  firing = source. Plan chain direction at script stage.
- **Big Word cards**: MAGMA, LAVA, ERUPTION are the naturals; EROSION
  if act 3 earns it. (Series shape: cold open → title → three acts →
  recap with chant + mind-blower + tease.)
- **Recap tease / next-time question**: ep 6 has NO arc canon — the
  tease is a real decision. Treatments propose one; synthesis picks;
  logged prominently for Mike (it is a promise to the audience). The
  ep-4 tease this episode must HONOR is in §1 (the dirt question +
  Pip's grading order).
- **Measured comedy standard**: laugh ≤25–30s target, MAX GAP ≤50s on
  spoken lines; any drawn-geometry stretch (cross-sections of the
  volcano WILL happen) needs a character beat inside it. The mechanism
  act should be the funniest act at writing stage.
- **Ensemble = replies, not lines.** Ember↔Rock, Ember↔Volcano,
  Sunny↔Narrator are the reply engines. Every speaking character wants
  something inside its one cast emotion.

## 7. Tone + engine rules (unchanged, binding)

No sarcasm a six-year-old can't parse; no scary peril — the eruption is
BIRTH, not danger (nothing and nobody gets hurt; the lava is glorious,
not menacing); no potty humour; corrections are gifts. Narrator + Sunny
on kokoro (letter-stretch allowed, no emotion/pauses); characters on
MiniMax (emotion as seasoning with stage-direction cites; NO all-caps
words in MiniMax line text — caps live in bubbles only; pause markers
only for scripted intra-line timing; sound-word spellings per-engine).
Volcano rule while asleep applies to every pre-wake shot (horizon,
never named, nobody looks); the WAKE is the licensed exception — that
is the whole point of five episodes of discipline.
