# EP 5 — Treatment B: "THE WAKE IS THE EPISODE"

Story-writer draft B from `ep5-brief.md`. Binding angle for this draft: the
**volcano is the co-lead**. Five episodes of horizon discipline are the
longest setup the series has ever run, and this treatment spends the whole
payoff on purpose: the wake is the act-1 climax, the eruption is the act-2
set piece, the island-building is the act-3 spectacle, and Sunny's wrongness
ceremony is staged at the eruption's peak — where the claim is most
undeniable and the one character who can hand down the verdict is the
subject of the frame. The butler-voiced volcano speaks seven times in the
whole episode and every line is a tent pole. The new hero is not introduced
NEAR the series event; she is born inside it — the first character in the
series whose entire existence is a consequence of the episode's set piece.
The impatience arc is argued from the same stance: Ember's clock ticks in
seconds, Rock's in centuries, and the volcano's in deep time — she spends
the episode inside the biggest patience in the world and mistakes it for
brokenness until she becomes part of it.

---

## 1. Logline + title candidates

**Logline.** The mountain that has held the horizon of every episode —
never named, never looked at — wakes up, and turns out to speak like a
butler. Out of his eruption shoots Ember, a brand-new drop of lava and the
fastest, most immediately-NOW character the series has had, who wants to be
big and wants it this minute. While the politest volcano in the world
builds a brand-new island underneath her one unhurried gesture at a time,
Ember gets stuck in the world's slowest traffic jam next to a boulder who
turns out to be her own future — and Sunny, who has been right for four
straight shows, finally claims the one fire on the planet that was never
his.

**Title candidates** (series pattern: hero's name + story noun):

1. **"Ember and the Mountain That Said Good Morning"** — preferred. Names
   the series event without the word "volcano" doing the work, and "said
   good morning" is the episode's first-line joke in the title.
2. "Ember and the Politest Volcano in the World" — names the butler.
3. "Ember and the Brand-New Island" — names the act-3 spectacle; flattest.
4. "Ember and the Volcano That Woke Up" — the literal one; safe.

**Hero name.** Keeping the brief's working name **Ember** — and arguing for
it: an ember is, by definition, a fast hot thing in the middle of becoming
a slow dark thing. The name IS the arc. Logged alternatives per brief:
*Spark* (one syllable, brighter, but a spark dies instead of hardening —
wrong arc), *Flick* (fast, sayable, no rock future in it), *Zing* (funny,
risks reading as a sound effect), *Zip* (best single syllable but rhymes
with Drip AND Pip — three rhyming heroes is an audio collision in any
shared scene, and act 3 shares scenes).

## 2. Hero spec — Ember

- **Who:** a brand-new drop of melted rock, she/her. Born on screen in
  Act 1, inside the magma chamber, mid-sentence, at maximum velocity.
  Youngest character the series has had (Ray was eight minutes old; Ember
  gets a birth scene).
- **Want:** to be big. Now. Not eventually, not gradually — now. She has
  been alive for forty seconds and considers most of that time wasted.
- **Attitude (heroes are comedians):** impatience with OPINIONS. Everything
  slow is, in her professional judgment, broken: the sealed vent is a
  broken door, the parked lava flow is broken traffic, the sea is "a lot
  of water with no schedule", and Rock — the first time she meets him — is
  "broken all the way through". She is never anxious and never bored: she
  is a tiny inspector condemning the entire geological world as defective,
  cheerfully, at speed. (The "___ is broken." runner, §5.)
- **The turn (argued from the stance):** the episode never teaches her
  that patience is a virtue. It shows her the actual price list. The three
  clocks are the argument: she is seconds, Rock is centuries, the volcano
  is deep time — and the volcano, the slowest of the three, is also the
  biggest thing she has ever seen, doing the most spectacular thing the
  series has ever shown, unhurried, mid-eruption, apologising for the
  noise. Fast things are small things: every fast thing she does in the
  episode is over in a second, and the one thing that happens to her
  INSTANTLY — cooling, the moment she hits the sea — is the thing that
  makes her permanent. Her discovery, said her way: she wanted big, big
  turns out to be measured in ten-thousands of years, and once she IS the
  newest layer of the newest island, slow stops feeling like stuck and
  starts feeling like long. "I used to be fast. Now I am ten thousand
  years long." Wrongness is never a comeuppance: nobody corrects her —
  the world just keeps being the size it is until she notices.
- **Casting (audition spec — new MiniMax voice, PROVISIONAL, measured
  overnight, logged for Mike's ear):** girl. Argued: (a) maximum register
  contrast with the mentor — the series' fastest, brightest, youngest
  voice against `Deep_Voice_Man` at 0.85 is the episode's Blue↔Red, and
  the top of that contrast range is a bright girl register; (b) collision
  avoidance — a fast boy voice at ≥1.05 shadows Blue's `Decent_Boy`, and
  Blue shares an act-3 scene with Ember (two fast boy voices in one scene
  is the ep-2 Beetle/Leaf bug waiting to happen); (c) Drip/Puff/Pip enter
  only in act 3 and the recap, at speeds ≤1.0, so the fast-girl lane is
  clear where it matters. Target speed 1.1 (suite's fastest); must sit
  audibly apart from `Lively_Girl`, `Exuberant_Girl`, `Inspirational_girl`
  at a six-year-old's ear. Audition trio: a machine-gun opinion line, a
  flat "broken" verdict, and the S21 self-answer (which must land content,
  not defeated). One cast emotion: `happy`; seasoning budget two lines
  (§5, stage directions cited). No all-caps words in any of her line text;
  her emphasis is repetition and speed, never caps.

## 3. The volcano — co-lead, seven lines, all tent poles

Casting per reservation: minimax `Elegant_Man`, **pitch −6**, proposed
speed 0.9, emotion `auto` on every line forever — the butler read is the
voice's job, not a seasoning's. Proposed identity gap: **48f of stillness
before every line** — the largest approach gap in the series (Red 16f);
the gap is the mountain deciding to move. He never gets a body walk; his
staging vocabulary is "the mountain moved slightly and that is enormous."
Never banter, never a reply under 48f, never a second sentence where one
will do. Pause needs are met by splitting clips and letting the timeline
hold, not by inline markers.

The complete line list (single digit, hard; this table IS his character):

| # | Scene | Line (MiniMax text, no caps) | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| V1 | S8 | "Good morning. I appear to have overslept." | FIRST LINE EVER — the series event. Returns Sunny's cold-open greeting four shows late. |
| V2 | S8 | "Do pardon the ash." | The butler register, confirmed; the first ash pop. |
| V3 | S9 | "Do excuse the noise. This is the loud part." | Reframes the eruption as scheduled work — the tone gate (construction, not catastrophe) carried by the character himself. |
| V4 | S13 | "If I may. The fire is mine. It came with the house." | The ceremony verdict's physics, in one line — the politest correction in series history. |
| V5 | S21 | "Presently." | His only reply to Ember; the slowest, politest clock in the world. |
| V6 | S22 | "I believe the garden is ready." | Hands the episode to the loop-closer (first soil → first seed). |
| V7 | S27 | "Good night." | Mirrors V1; the scenery now says goodnight. He is NOT going back to sleep — Narrator closes the ambiguity. |

Everything else he "says" is geology: rumbles, steam, one slight movement
per act. Ember addresses him constantly; the engine is her speed against
his 48f silences — nine lines aimed at him, two answered, and the ratio is
the joke.

## 4. Act-by-act beat sheet

Volcano staging: on the measured horizon, continuously visible,
unnamed and unlooked-at through S1–S2 per the volcano rule; **S3 is the
licensed exception and the series turn** — from S3 on, looking at him is
legal forever. Laugh engine named per stretch as **Engine:**.

### COLD OPEN

- **S1 — The last quiet morning.** Beach below the horizon-line volcano,
  dawn. The kid (silhouette, silent, fifth episode running — sixth
  counting drip-fork) builds a sandcastle. Narrator re-poses the ep-4 end
  card, near-verbatim: last time, somebody asked what dirt is actually
  made of — "Today, the answer is going to introduce itself." Sunny rises:
  **"GOOD MORNING, EVERYBODY!"** / **"I invented mornings!"** (series
  constant, fired as the pair, five-for-five) — and takes credit for the
  lovely warm sand: **"You're welcome! HA! HA!"** (firing 1). *Engine:
  Sunny's ego + Narrator deadpan; the sandcastle plants the silent
  builder-parallel runner (§5).*
- **S2 — The knock.** A sound nobody has ever heard: enormous, deep,
  polite — once. The sandcastle wobbles; the kid pats it back together.
  Sunny, instantly, at full wattage — the banked wake-claim, fired with
  its banked direction (full confidence, no doubt anywhere in it):
  **"OH! That one is me as well! HA! HA!"** Narrator, 0.92 flat: "Nobody
  had asked. He answered anyway." No verdict, no doubt cast — the claim
  just sits there, armed. *Engine: the three-episode-banked claim landing
  at last + the deadpan that declines to rule on it.*
- **S3 — The rules end.** Thin smoke on the summit. Narrator names the
  series discipline out loud, then breaks it: "For four shows there has
  been a mountain on that horizon. Nobody looked at it. Nobody named it.
  Those were the rules." [held beat, 30f] "The rules are over." The camera
  does the thing it has never done: it turns and faces the volcano head
  on, full frame. *Engine: the meta-payoff itself — for the kids, the
  first-ever LOOK at the thing; for the adults, five episodes of
  discipline cashing.* **TITLE CARD** over the volcano, full frame.

### ACT 1 — DOWNSTAIRS (the wake set piece; Ember born inside it)

- **S4 — The world's basement.** Cross-section descent: under the grass,
  under the rock, down to where it glows. **Big Word card: MAGMA.**
  Pedagogy, stated plainly and PLANTED for the ceremony: down here it is
  so hot that rock is melted — and the heat is the world's own, left over
  from the day the world was made. It has been down here the whole time.
  (The audience now knows the verdict three scenes before Sunny claims
  the fire — kids get to be ahead of the Sun.) Character beats inside the
  drawn geometry (diagram-sag rule): the magma crowd — dozens of
  identical glow-drops with faces, the episode's free crowd — and one
  muffled interruption from very far above: Sunny, through a kilometre of
  rock: "If anyone down there needs light, I am available!" (Sunny
  interrupts a scene not about him — the one place he has never been;
  the offer is true, he is available; marked CUTTABLE). *Engine:
  narrator-deadpan pedagogy + Sunny's long-distance ego.*
- **S5 — Ember, mid-sentence.** A drop separates from the glow already
  talking — fast, bright, opinionated, twelve words into a complaint when
  we meet her. First entrance at maximum velocity: she laps the chamber
  while introducing herself. Verdict on her birthplace: "Nothing down
  here moves. It is all broken." (runner firing 1). She addresses the
  chamber — the volcano — directly and gets 48 frames of nothing.
  *Engine: motormouth vs the politest silence in the world.*
- **S6 — The broken door.** Ember finds the sealed vent and inspects it
  like management: "The door is broken." (runner firing 2). She tries
  speed — ricochets off. Tries again. The magma crowd watches with mild
  interest; nobody else down here has ever been in a hurry. *Engine:
  impatience physical comedy + the crowd's faces (zero-cost ensemble).*
- **S7 — Warm things rise.** The whole chamber begins to move — UP.
  Narrator, cross-episode rhyme: "Warm things rise. You learned that in
  show two. It is also true downstairs." Ember, thrilled, finally in
  traffic that moves: "Now we are moving. Let's go, let's go, let's go."
  *Engine: Ember's speed rewarded exactly once, so the act break can take
  it away.*
- **S8 — THE FIRST LINE (act-1 climax; the series event).** The summit
  puffs; cut wide to the surface: Sunny mid-brag, the kid mid-pat,
  everything frozen; 45f of held nothing. Then, from the mountain,
  enormous, unhurried, courteous: **"Good morning. I appear to have
  overslept."** [held 60f — the world reels] **"Do pardon the ash."** —
  one small ash pop, on the button. Sunny's world tilts: scenery has
  never greeted him back: "GOOD MORNING?! It — said good morning. TO ME!"
  Narrator, flat: "The mountain said good morning. It has excellent
  manners. Hold on to something." *Engine: the biggest single beat of the
  episode after the ceremony — the butler reveal — plus Sunny's rocked
  worldview.* **ACT BREAK.**

### ACT 2 — THE LOUD PART (eruption; Rock; the ceremony)

- **S9 — The eruption begins.** Fountains, glow, glory — staged as
  construction from the first frame: nothing flees, nothing is harmed,
  the world leans in. **Big Word card: ERUPTION.** The volcano, over his
  own fireworks: **"Do excuse the noise. This is the loud part."**
  Narrator: he is not exploding — he is building; this is what a
  construction site sounds like when the site is the world. *Engine:
  butler vs spectacle — the bigger the fireworks, the smaller his
  apology.*
- **S10 — Ember out (and renamed mid-flight).** Ember rides the fountain
  into open sky — her best second ever — and the Narrator renames her in
  transit: inside, she was magma; outside, she is lava. Same stuff. New
  name. **Big Word card: LAVA**, staged side by side with the MAGMA card
  and one arrow — the same-stuff-new-name gag as a picture. Ember,
  offended at speed: "Nobody told me there was a name change. Was there
  paperwork? I was busy." *Engine: the rename gag (the joke IS the
  pedagogy) + peak velocity before the traffic jam.*
- **S11 — The traffic jam.** The flow front crusts and slows (honest:
  flows skin over and stall); Ember, three seconds from the sea, parks.
  Beside the flow, warm at last, sits ROCK. Narrator: **"Different show.
  Same rock."** (firing 1 of 2, 0.92 flat). Rock, feeling the warmth of
  a lava flow arrive next to him after centuries of sunshine: **"Ohh
  yeah. That is the stuff."** (re-fire 1 — the ep-2 recording via cache
  migration; same take, hotter cause). Ember's verdict: "This traffic is
  broken." (runner firing 3). Rock: "It is resting." *Engine: the
  fastest character in the series parked next to the slowest, both
  completely certain.*
- **S12 — The unmoving countdown (engine source scene).** The two-hander
  proper. Ember: "How long until I am a mountain?" Rock — 24f of
  geological consideration — **"Ten thousand years."** (CHAIN SOURCE,
  firing 1; sameAs discipline, earliest firing is the source). Ember,
  recalibrating: "How long if I hurry?" Rock: "No." Then the reveal,
  Rock's register, shortest sentences: "I came out of there." / Ember:
  "You were lava?" / **"I was fast once."** — Ember is literally Rock's
  childhood; Rock is Ember's future, sitting right there, content.
  Ember's horror: "How long have you been sitting here?" Rock: "Since I
  stopped." *Engine: the countdown that never counts down + the mirror
  reveal. (Why this is NOT the ep-3 gag re-skinned: §5.)*
- **S13 — THE CEREMONY, part 1: the claim.** The eruption at full glory
  behind them — the whole sky lit, the most undeniable picture the claim
  will ever get. Sunny, four episodes of zero wrongness at his back,
  banked verbatim, full confidence: **"I did that."** Held, 60f — nobody
  answers; the eruption roars politely on. Narrator, banked verbatim,
  gently: **"No. He really didn't."** Held. Then the volcano — 48f, the
  mountain deciding to move — the politest correction in series history:
  **"If I may. The fire is mine. It came with the house."** Narrator
  translation, the S4 plant cashing: deep down, the world is still warm
  from the day it was made. That heat was never anybody's gift. It is
  the world's own — the one warm thing on the planet that was never
  Sunny's. *Engine: the five-episode payoff itself; the laugh is the
  butler taking the verdict off the Narrator's hands.*
- **S14 — THE CEREMONY, part 2: the fuse closes.** Narrator: "A long
  time ago, I told you something. I said: one day, Sunny will be wrong
  about something." — **36f** (byte-mirror of the ep-2 gap; kokoro is
  deterministic, so re-speaking the ep-2 sentence verbatim is the
  identical take for free) — **"It is today."** Then THE SILENCE:
  **180f, six full seconds — the longest silence the series has ever
  held** (previous record 165f). Nothing enters it: the eruption glows,
  Sunny hangs still, Ember — for the first time in her life — does not
  speak. This is Sunny's stunned beat, the series' first. Then, quietly
  (his second-quietest line ever, after ep-4's "I know."): "...The fire
  is not mine." [beat] And the recovery, AS SUNNY, swelling back to full
  wattage on truths: "But the sea that will cool it is warm because of
  me! The sky it is climbing through is bright because of me! The fire
  is one thing. I am all the other things! HA! HA!" Narrator button,
  0.92 flat, kind: "Most other things. He is most other things." —
  his infallibility is dead; his ego is visibly fine. *Engine: the long
  fuse + the recovery-as-Sunny (the kids' laugh is the bounce-back; the
  parents' is "It is today.").* **ACT BREAK.**

### ACT 3 — THE NEWEST PLACE IN THE WORLD (island building; erosion; the loop)

- **S15 — The surge.** The flow surges (honest: stalled flows surge when
  the crust breaks) and Ember goes to sea. Steam everywhere — and the
  fastest thing that ever happened to her happens: the sea skins her in
  seconds. Ember, seasoned `surprised` (stage direction: her outside
  hardens mid-word): "Wait. Am I rock? Already? That was — fast?"
  Narrator: "Cooling is quick. Growing is the slow part." New rock,
  brand-new land, the newest place in the world. *Engine: the reversal —
  the one instant thing in geology happens to the character who demanded
  instant.*
- **S16 — ROLL CALL, firing 1 (greeting).** From her new black rock,
  Ember sees what the horizon has been hiding all series: a queue of
  near-identical islands trailing away from the volcano, newest to
  oldest — the last one barely a sandbar. Cheerfully, oldest of the
  strangers last: "Hi, Isla! Hi, Islet! Hi, other Island! Hi, Isle the
  third! ...Hi, Sandy." (Sandy is the sandbar — the name does the
  erosion pedagogy inside the naming.) Narrator, one flat explanatory
  line: "The volcano has done this before. Many times." Unbothered,
  unseasoned button — Ember: "Big family." Nobody replies inside the
  naming. *Engine: the series-signature roll call, picked because the
  joke IS the lesson: the archipelago is the volcano's CV.*
- **S17 — The island grows (and Rock moves).** Eruption by eruption,
  layer by layer, the new island stacks under Ember — she narrates her
  own construction impatiently ("Faster. You are building me wrong.").
  Ember: "How long until the island is finished?" Rock — 60f —
  **"Ten thousand years."** (chain firing 2). And then the series does
  something to Rock: the surge has rafted him down the slope (honest:
  flows carry rafted boulders, unmelted — big rocks take far longer to
  melt than a flow has), and he grinds gently ashore on the new island.
  Rock, after centuries, with total calm, hugest event of his life in
  the smallest sentence of the episode: **"I moved."** *Engine: the
  countdown holding steady while everything changes + Rock's deadpan
  landmark. Even the ground travels — the quiet thesis, on legs.*
- **S18 — The demolition crew.** Rain and wind arrive to take the OLD
  islands apart, cheerfully. Drip rides in on the first shower —
  standing entrance, fourth identical firing, cache-migrated from the
  approved `a1_31_drip` take: **"Hi! It's me! I'm the weather!"**
  Narrator: **"Different show. Same drop."** (firing 2 of 2, fresh
  blank). Puff's entrance is his catch-phrase, exactly once,
  cache-migrated from wind `a1_40_puff` (sidesteps the caps-in-text
  fresh-draw risk): **"You can't see me. But you can FEEL me."** —
  perfect for wind erosion, and his "Different show" is skipped per the
  ep-4 ruling (an invisible character cannot be pointed at). Their
  job, stated with pride: they turn mountains into beaches, one grain
  at a time, and it takes approximately forever — the demolition is as
  slow as the construction. Ember: "How long to break a mountain?"
  Rock — 96f, his longest gap — **"Ten thousand years."** (chain firing
  3, final: same number in both directions; the number is the whole
  subject). Sunny claims the crew, correctly — his recovered
  half-claim demonstrated on screen: "My rain! My wind! The demolition
  runs on me! You're welcome! HA! HA!" (firing 2; true — his weather
  does the crumbling). **Big Word card: EROSION** (earned). *Engine:
  cheerful demolition + the constant number + Sunny's legitimate
  bounce-back.*
- **S19 — What dirt is actually made of.** The answer scene. The chain
  of islands is the diagram and it is made of scenery the show already
  owns: newest island black rock, older ones sandier, older ones GREEN

  Narrator: "So was the island. Yesterday." *Engine: the recipe

- **S20 — Blue, first to the newest place in the world (+ Violet).**

- **S21 — The turn (held in silence).** Dusk; the eruption settles to a

- **S22 — The garden is ready.** First light on the new island. Sunny

## 9. Estimated runtime and where the risk is

**Estimate: 15–18 minutes.** 27 scenes; fewer lines than ep 4 but far more
held air (the silences ledger alone is ~25 seconds of deliberate nothing),
plus two set pieces (wake, eruption) and a spectacle act.

**Risks, ranked:**

1. **Laugh cadence through the volcano-centric stretches.** Scarcity is
   the character, so the co-lead cannot carry the laugh clock — the
   ceremony block (S13–S14) is the episode's longest deliberate solemn
   stretch. Protection is structural: Sunny's confident claim, the
   butler's "If I may.", and the recovery aria are all inside it, so the
   measured gap on spoken lines stays under ~40s; but this is a
   self-score, and the rule says recompute from the manifest — flagging
   S4 (cross-section), S13–S14, and S22 as the three stretches to audit
   first at script stage.
2. **The 180f record silence.** Six seconds is a phone-screen eternity;
   it is also the whole point of five episodes. The hold has motion
   without sound (eruption glow) and Sunny visibly still in it. Fallback
   trim is 168f — it must stay the longest silence the series has ever
   held (>165f) or the stance loses its biggest claim.
3. **The new voice.** Ember is ~46 recordings on an overnight-auditioned,
   PROVISIONAL casting. Mitigation per ep-4 precedent: one constant in
   `narration.mjs`, chain sources identified before generation, recast =
   re-buy.
4. **Act 1 is a new set (inside the volcano) with drawn geometry.**
   Ember is born one scene into it and the magma crowd has faces;
   Sunny's long-distance interruption is the third beat and is marked
   CUTTABLE, not load-bearing.
5. **One-word chant clips + "Isla/Islet" pronunciations** are the known
   highest-TTS-risk class — fallback respellings and a Narrator-led
   chant fallback get pre-written at script stage per the ep-4 rule.

## 10. Deviations from the brief, flagged loudly

1. **The ceremony is staged at the END OF ACT 2, at the eruption's peak**
   (placement was explicitly a treatment decision; here is the argument):
   (a) undeniability peaks when the fire is at full glory in plain sight —
   the claim and its refutation share one frame; (b) the volcano is the
   subject of the shot, so the verdict's physics costs one butler line
   instead of a diagram; (c) act 3 then plays Sunny's recovery ON SCREEN
   (S18 legitimate claim, S22 inversion, S25 half-claim) — the tone
   guardrail "his ego survives" is demonstrated for a full act rather
   than asserted in a button; (d) the record silence sits on the
   brightest picture the series has ever drawn, which keeps six seconds
   of nothing watchable; (e) an act-3 ceremony would share its silence
   with the island, the loop, the roll call and the tease — this episode
   has one biggest-ever silence and it should own its scene.
2. **Sunny's banked "That is not me." inversion is SPENT** (S22, verbatim,
   once). Argument: the ceremony kills his infallibility; the inversion
   is the proof it took — Sunny voluntarily allocating credit is the
   funniest possible evidence that he survived, and it converts the
   ceremony from an event into a permanent character upgrade. If the
   showrunner wants it banked for a later episode, S22 plays without it
   (the scene's spine is the seed) — one-line cut.
3. **Rock's catch-phrase fires twice** (S11 warmth, S21 the grade) off
   ONE recording — cache migration from wind + in-episode alias.
   Argument: ep-2's own canon shape was two firings, one recording,
   with nothing happening in between; ep 5 reproduces the shape with an
   entire island in between. The second firing is the mentor's verdict
